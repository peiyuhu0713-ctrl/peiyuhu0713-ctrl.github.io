Research

Generative AI Intervention & Digital Imagination

Independent Researcher (OSF-preregistered) · Mar 2026 – Present · github.com/peiyuhu0713-ctrl

  • Built a quantitative framework to test whether generative-model safety/alignment constraints are monotonically beneficial, operationalising creative outcomes as measurable constructs — semantic alignment, visual diversity, repair cost (prompt iterations needed to reach intent), a validity threshold for acceptable output, and diversity cost (representational range lost as constraints tighten).
  • Designed three pilot rounds and a pre-registered main experiment over SDXL constraint conditions, prompt-stratified test sets, and placebo controls; generated and evaluated 2,500+ images, identifying a non-monotonic inverted-U relationship between constraint strength and creative diversity, peaking near the model's default setting.
  • Built the full Python / PyTorch / Hugging Face pipeline on cloud GPU (AutoDL): automated generation, a quality-control gate, scoring with a 4-bit-quantised LLaVA-1.5-7B, and CLIP / LPIPS metric computation.
  • Ran stratified robustness checks and attention ablations via PyTorch forward hooks against a placebo-module control, to separate attention-level effects from prompt-level differences.
  • Designed a qualitative arm — semi-structured creator interviews — to map model boundaries (soft / cost / trade-off / persistent) and how repair burden and cultural-representation gaps fall unequally across users.
